Read before arrival

Four details that change how you pack and plan

  • No aquatics: Camp Lawton has no pool or waterfront.
  • Carry-in gear: Vehicles remain in the designated parking area.
  • Fire restrictions: Conditions can change without warning; always plan a no-flame alternative.
  • Medications: Prescription medications go to the Camp Health Officer at check-in.

Summer Camp 2027

Official Registration is Open

Official registration for Camp Lawton Summer Camp 2027 is now open on Black Pug. Secure your unit or provisional spot for Week 1 (June 6–12) or Week 2 (June 13–19).

Official RegistrationVisit the official registration site at scoutingevent.com/011-ScoutCamp2027 to register.

Scouts: $400 in-council or $450 out-of-council. Adults: $100. Youth deposit: $40. Early bird savings and payment options are listed on the official event page.

Each week is limited to 150 youth. Merit badge registration opens February 1, 2027.
